What it's for

Crypto revenue doesn't fit in a spreadsheet.

Payments arrive in ETH, SOL and stablecoins. Prices move by the minute. By month end, nobody knows what a payment was worth when it landed. Firebook is the ledger your product writes to as money moves, so the books stay current on their own.

  • Founders and finance

    See income, expenses and net in USD for any period, with a breakdown by currency.

  • Engineering

    Record entries from your backend with an API key. No CSV exports, no manual reconciliation.

  • Operations

    Tag entries by client, vendor or plan, and filter every statement by those tags.

  • Your accountant

    Every entry keeps its original amount, its USD value at the time, and the metadata you attached.

How it works

From payment to statement in one request.

  1. 1

    Create an organization and an API key

    Sign up, name your organization, and generate a key for your backend. Invite your team when you're ready.

  2. 2

    Send an entry when money moves

    Post the amount, currency and type. Firebook values USD and ETH at the current market price, or you can pass your own usdValue.

  3. 3

    Read your books

    Totals, daily cash flow and per-currency breakdowns update right away, and every view can be filtered by tag.

Features

Everything you need to keep crypto books, nothing you don't.

  • USD value at the time

    Each entry stores what it was worth when it was recorded, priced live from on-chain markets.

  • Tags and metadata

    Attach key:value tags and invoice IDs, then filter entries and statements by them.

  • Statements and cash flow

    Income, expenses and net for this month, the last 30 or 90 days, day by day and per currency.

  • Backdating and your own prices

    Record past payments with a date, and pass the exact USD value when you already know it.

  • Teams and organizations

    Keep separate books per company and invite up to 15 teammates to each organization.

  • Scoped API keys

    Create a key per service and revoke it instantly. Keys only write to their own organization.
